## 4. Practical conditional knowledge for future design (not SKILL approval)

The following are **documented research considerations**, not performance-certified rules:

| Design dimension | Supported bounded use | Required guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Category and format selection | Model Category → Niche → Sub-niche, with independent format, audience, language, duration and viewer-intent constraints | No universal three-act/hook-seconds/pacing quota |
| Factual/script integrity | Source-locate consequential claims and distinguish predictions, observations, quotes and dramatizations | Avoid fabricated nonfiction dialogue, fake historical timestamps or forecast certainty |
| Script voice/readability | Assess speakability and accessibility against intended audience and delivery | Written readability or captions cannot alone prove naturally spoken prosody |
| Hook and story structures | Consider alternative openings, curiosity, stakes, exposition and payoff **as optional hypotheses** | Urgent weather/news warnings may require service-first disclosure; no artificial information concealment |
| Format-specific conventions | Film screenplay, live sport, presenter tutorial and narrated documentary require different deliverables | Do not import unrelated formatting standards or live-radio timers as universal requirements |
| Performance measurement | Keep public view counts, Shorts playback starts, engaged views, impressions, CTR, AVD and retention distinct | Do not infer retention or success probability from editorial rubrics without suitable owner analytics or experiments |
| Multilingual storytelling | Localize factual meaning/register based on actual source evidence and audience | Do not enforce historically FAILED categorical language rules such as F-092 |

These considerations are suitable for a **research/requirements brief only**, and must be checked again for a specifically scoped future implementation. The original corrected synthesis, not this report, remains the authoritative source for K wording.
