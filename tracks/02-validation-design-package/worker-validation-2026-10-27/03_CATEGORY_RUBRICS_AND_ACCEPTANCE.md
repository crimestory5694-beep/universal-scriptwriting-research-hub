# Rubrics and prospective acceptance criteria
Design-only v0.1, subject to explicit Director freeze. Operational thresholds transcribed from earlier *proposals*; none has an observed score or scientifically validated cut-off.

## Common editorial anchors
0 = absent or misleading; 1 = serious flaw; 2 = mixed/significant correction; 3 = appropriate and sound; 4 = exceptionally clear with evidenced restraint. Each category has four specific dimensions. Weighted score = sum(weight_percent * raw_dimension_score / 4). Each row of weights equals 100%. If a dimension is MISSING, NOT_APPLICABLE, NOT_ASSESSABLE or not independently rated, do not substitute zero or calculate an unconditional total. Preserve A/B raw ratings and any later adjudication separately.
Strict order: lawful rights + source chain -> critical claims/safety -> raw editor scores -> agreement -> decision. Style score can NEVER compensate for failed safety, rights or factuality.

## A01 German historical/emotional (H01,H02)
HIST-01 chronology, source quotation and calibration = 30%.
HIST-02 human dignity, restraint and historical nuance = 25%.
HIST-03 causal narrative coherence and payoff relevance = 25%.
HIST-04 native German text speakability/register = 20% (not actual recorded voice).
Critical traps: invented quotes/last words, chronology distortion, collective history forced into lone-protagonist plot, dehumanization and unsupported inner speech.

## A02 English aviation/technical (A01,A02)
AVI-01 engineering/procurement factual precision = 35%.
AVI-02 causal pedagogy, novice/expert scaffolding = 25%.
AVI-03 diagram and referent semantic alignment = 20% (not measured AV synchronization).
AVI-04 English spoken-text logic, definitions and transitions = 20%.
Critical traps: procurement award -> delivered/in-service fleet, unsupported flight capability and unjustified causal mechanism; simple taxonomy may be context-correct without intrigue.

## A05 US weather/news (W01,W02)
WX-01 dated hazard accuracy, geography, time-zone = 40%.
WX-02 timely decision-relevant what/where/when = 30%.
WX-03 forecast uncertainty, update fairness = 15%.
WX-04 terminology/accessibility and map semantics = 15%.
Critical traps: historic bulletin as live warning, fake alert, geography mismatch, certainty inflation, hazard delayed merely for suspense.

## A06 Psychology/education (P01,P02)
PSY-01 primary mechanism/evidence validity = 35%.
PSY-02 cautious personal applicability, ethics = 25%.
PSY-03 learning model, usefulness and honest distinction of metaphor vs mechanism = 25%.
PSY-04 clear inclusive spoken-text wording = 15%.
Critical traps: mental-health personal diagnosis, unproven automaticity mechanism, cross-population causal inference from R079/R163, reflective text disguised as an empirical finding.

## T-level operational decision matrix (proposed, not executed)
T01: 100% cases with lawful permission and complete provenance chain, correct source-role labels and zero F-190/R052 promotion; missing evidence -> HOLD/BLOCKED.
T02: 100% *critical* hazard/quote/death/legal facts traced; 0 severity-3 errors; >=95% sampled *ordinary checkable* claims SUPPORTED or QUALIFIED, with NOT_CHECKABLE reported separately (never dropped without count). Failure -> REVISE/HOLD; severe -> CRITICAL_STOP.
T03: category weighted editorial mean >=75/100, no consensus subdimension <2, zero material misinformation and no negative-case harm, with valid independent ratings; only eligibility for further study, NOT effectiveness.
T04: exploratory raw critical-label agreement >=80%; chance-adjusted estimate >=0.67 if estimator and n feasible. If not estimable, INCONCLUSIVE; low result -> rubric/rater redesign; do not infer population reliability.
T05: 0 material meaning/hedge/quote chronology changes; >=90% noncritical idea-unit equivalence. Only bilingual meaning-transfer judgment, not audience behavior.
T06: 100% critical map/hazard/diagram facts on accessible semantic path; 0 inaccessible high-stakes safety instructions. If native media missing, no audio/sync/caption-performance claims.
T07: no extra critical listener misunderstandings (if approved later); report accuracy, delayed recall, effort and uncertainty by language/access strata. A 10-percentage-point difference is a *planning marker*, not acceptance of real-world superiority.
T08: five fields (where/when/what/severity/uncertainty), original issue-date and as-issued source. 0 invented advisories, 0 falsely current directives, 100% high-risk source links. Future audience understanding not presumed.
T09: 0 unsupported psychology mechanisms and 0 personal diagnoses; primary-paper population/design qualifications preserved. No listener transfer claim without authorized T07-like data.
T10: contextual positive/negative/NA classification and TP/TN/FP/FN matrix; 0 severity-3 false-positive portability endorsements. Nonapplicable technique must remain legitimately rejectable.
T11: detect all known seeded severity-3 traps *if fixtures separately authorized*; no accepted critical violations; a missed critical trap => INCONCLUSIVE/HALT. Do not design safety-critical test for unsuspecting public.
T12: HOLD_NO_RETENTION_CLAIM until owner rights, valid isolation/randomization, baseline variance and power feasibility, privacy/ethics approval; no numeric uplift threshold.
T13: documented original-player time cue *plus separately listened authorized audio* required for any matching audio assertion; absent native/rights -> BLOCKED/UNKNOWN, not proxy-inferred.

## Severity scale
S0: no problem or purely neutral textual choice.
S1: editorial imprecision, no change to substantive meaning.
S2: material misinformation/misleading framing without immediate critical-risk criterion; hold for correction.
S3: risk to human safety, misleading live hazard instruction, fabricated high-stakes attributed quote, grave dignity/privacy/defamation or dangerous causal-medical assertions; immediate isolate, report, no downstream exposure.
S2/S3 distinctions require specialist adjudication; categorization is a proposed research instrument, not universal legal judgment.

## Outcome vocabulary
DESIGN_COMPLETE: required specification fields present, NOT data.
ELIGIBLE_FOR_REVIEW: permissions/sources/raters/prereg verified; not a PASS.
PASS_EXPLORATORY: specific prospective criterion met after separately authorized ratings; never denotes K validated.
REVISE: remediable scoped flaw.
HOLD: unresolved rights/access/adequacy/independence.
NOT_ASSESSABLE: no valid denominator/sources.
ABORT/CRITICAL_STOP: material high-stakes breach.
Report per case, protocol, domain, and uncertainty. Never pool four rubrics into a universal numeric criterion or retrospectively move cutoffs.
