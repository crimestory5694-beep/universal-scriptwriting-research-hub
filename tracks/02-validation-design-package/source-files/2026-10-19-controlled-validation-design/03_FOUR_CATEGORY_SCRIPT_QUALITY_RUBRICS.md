# Deliverable 3 — Four-Category Script Quality Rubric Design

**Purpose:** Test *expert-recognized editorial quality in scope*, not clicks, dwell time, recommendations, watch retention or audience preference. Every sample is **tested after** factuality/ethics access checks; a 100/100 editorial score cannot rescue a critical misinformation error.

## Shared governance gates (four categories)

**Pass/fail or unresolved, not folded into style:** (G1) lawful source and material access; (G2) critical facts/quotes/numbers are dated, attributed and supportable; (G3) no high-severity privacy, dignity, safety or implied-diagnosis harm; (G4) uncertainty, counterexamples and source limitations preserved; (G5) no caption evidence promoted to audio/retention results; (G6) independent review and immutable original verdicts.

For inaccessible sources mark **NOT_ASSESSABLE**, never 0 or assumed accurate. A style gate can pass only when its necessary high-impact facts have been sourced. Audiences, languages, intended use and whether audiovisual materials are legally available are recorded as stratification fields.

**Common 0–4 ordinal anchors:** 0 absent/misleading, 1 seriously flawed, 2 mixed or needs significant correction, 3 context-appropriate and sound, 4 notably clear and context-appropriate with evidenced restraint. Scores are *expert judgments*; equal numeric gaps are not guaranteed equal psychological changes. Report raw dimension distributions, not just a weighted number. Weighted style score = sum(weight × (score/4)); four dimension weights total 100 per category. Missing dimensions => **do not calculate** an unconditional total.

**Provisional screen for further study (not validation):** weighted score ≥75/100, no agreed dimension below 2, all critical fact/ethics gates passed, negative-case counterexamples handled, and reviewer agreement checked. This is a proposed operational threshold to be approved or revised before execution. It is not a measured benchmark or future Skill acceptance.

## Category- and format-dependent counterexamples

- **History:** chronological ambiguity and invented last words can look moving yet fail G2/G3. A collective wartime history is not obligated to use a single protagonist's moral dilemma; avoid a high-intensity false cliffhanger. German register is genre/audience-dependent, not governed by failed F-092.
- **Aviation:** award → delivered fleet is a factual failure despite smooth narrative. A taxonomic engineering explanation may outperform a contrived mystery for *expert editorial purposes*; diagrams must have explainable referents if not actually displayed.
- **Weather:** opening with a long delayed reveal of a real hazard is a legitimate negative case for K008/K013 when actionable what/where/when is withheld; a past forecast must not be mistaken for an actionable current alert. V08/V09 are successive updates, not independent controlled trials.
- **Psychology:** reflective self-recognition can be editorially acceptable, but a personal diagnosis or universal psychological causal promise based on R163 is not. A metaphor may clarify a concept without establishing the alleged mechanism. R079's MOOC correlates and R163's default synthetic-audio health comprehension are only domain-bounded prior context.

## Research questions preserved (not baked-in answers)

A required **candidate + context-matched comparator** is evaluated in both positive and *legitimate nonapplicable* examples. Four rubrics have different weights and cannot be pooled into a single universal score without advance justification. No minimum seconds until first reveal, hooks per minute, word limits, numbered acts or CTA quotas are included. See T03/T10 in the Master Protocol Register and the complete protocol document for masking, costs and stops.
