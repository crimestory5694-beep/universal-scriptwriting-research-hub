# Deliverable 7 — Experimental YouTube Retention Research: Feasibility Only

**Protocol:** T12; existing P06. **Feasibility class C: genuinely owner-controlled analytics required. Not initiated.** None of the 12 caption-based V observations, public view counts or R079/R163 observational findings supplies a script-treatment retention effect.

## Causal target to specify separately (if future approved)

Define one **script-only contrast** on the same bounded source facts, and predeclare whether the outcome is platform-defined *average view duration*, *average percentage viewed*, *completion*, or a precisely scoped retention-curve property. Exposures and denominators must match YouTube's current actual reporting semantics. CTR/views are distinct reach/packaging outcomes and must not substitute for retention. Report exposure window, platform-defined units, repeated viewers and clip/video length eligibility.

## Minimal validity questions before committing resources

1. Does the owner authorize historical and prospective **nonpublic Analytics** data use, with privacy minimization and permitted export/API path?
2. Is exposure to one script variant credibly randomized at viewer level (not usually controllable merely by uploading two public videos), or is the design a quasi-experimental observational comparison with unavoidable allocation confounding?
3. Can both versions be matched on **topic, title, thumbnail, audience, timing, distribution, visual assets, narrator, duration and promotion** while changing primarily the script treatment? Some matching controls may be technically infeasible on the platform.
4. Is there a baseline eligible-viewer count, variance and feasible duration to run an **a priori power calculation** for the prespecified minimal worthwhile effect, with clustering/repeated exposure accounted for?
5. Will one primary comparison, analysis plan, confidence interval and multiple-comparison strategy be frozen before examining post-treatment data? Is the owner prepared to report null and adverse outcomes?

## Decision matrix

| Situation | Admissible interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| No owner consent or no authentic Analytics exports | **BLOCKED**; cannot test retention |
| Owner data but no valid allocation or treatment isolation | **OBSERVATIONAL ASSOCIATION ONLY**, no causal uplift |
| Allocation viable, but insufficient effective sample/power | **FEASIBILITY HOLD**; do not infer absence of effect |
| Valid treatment allocation, permission, sufficient power and credible controls | Seek **separate preregistered experimental approval** |

**Specific confounds:** platform recommendation and first-viewer selection, audience intent, publishing order, seasonality, title/thumbnail changes, different video length (completion denominator changes), sponsor placement, audio delivery, channel growth and repeated exposures. Raw public V01 vs V02 differences and same-channel V08/V09 cannot identify script effectiveness. R079 MOOC length observations and R163 health TTS comprehensions cannot set YouTube causal lift estimates.

**Costs/ethics:** Not priced or committed. Requires channel-owner time, qualified statistician, potentially high sample size, permitted aggregation, appropriate notices and owner data protection controls. No fabricated effect size, required hook window, universal uplift or promised access. The Research Director must explicitly authorize C later even if all A tests receive a favorable verdict.
