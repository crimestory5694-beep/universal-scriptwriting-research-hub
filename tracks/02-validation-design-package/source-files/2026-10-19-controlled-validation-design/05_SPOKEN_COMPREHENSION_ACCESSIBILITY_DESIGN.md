# Deliverable 5 — Spoken Comprehension & Accessibility Test Design

**Study IDs:** T05 (bilingual desk meaning), T06 (accessibility and semantic visual desk), T07 (future consented listeners), T08 (weather-specific decisions), T09 (psychology teaching mechanism). Linked priorities P07/P08/P09/P15.

## Separate what experts can judge from what listeners can demonstrate

**A / expert desk review only:** Native German and native English reviewers inspect *written* meaning, spoken plausibility of names/jargon, semantic audio-description plans and intended caption information. These ratings cannot establish that a human narrator reads naturally or that listeners understood, because original source audio from the historical video analysis was not verified. Experts do not guess pause lengths or speech cadence.

**B / consented listening (later, pending approval):** Create matched short human-voice recordings only from content owned/licensed for the study; record delivery/prosody, quality and equipment. Recruit opt-in adults in relevant English/German/novice contexts only if approved; recruit disabled accessibility participants only with accessible design, informed consent and appropriate accommodations. TTS can be a separately named comparison condition only if the research question justifies it, never used as surrogate for R163 generalizability.

## Future listener protocol (NOT EXECUTED)

- **Material:** one source packet per case and source-faithful audio conditions; semantic visuals only when licensed. Compare candidate and comparator on the same factual claims, meaning, speaker parameters and approximate information load. Equal nominal word count alone will not guarantee matching.
- **Population:** distinguish native German DACH viewers for history, English aviation novices/enthusiasts, English US weather users with region knowledge recorded, and English psychology learners. Do not combine languages/cases into one treatment effect without advance model justification.
- **Allocation:** if an actual comparison is approved, specify unit of randomization and sequence, conceal where practical, and counterbalance order and practice effects. Mark the study `observational` if allocation is not randomized.
- **Outcome:** item-keyed immediate factual comprehension and task success, explicit where/when/what uncertainty questions for weather, mechanism and counterexample recognition for psychology, referent recognition for aviation, factual/ethical nuance for history; delayed recall only with an explicit interval plan. Record incorrect answers *and* `DON'T KNOW` options.
- **Accessibility:** provide accessible transcript/captions, non-speech cues, descriptions of essential graphic information and non-color-only references; document actual user access paths. Different access conditions must be modeled separately rather than assumed equivalent.
- **Analysis:** distributions and rates by category/language/expertise; descriptive uncertainty intervals, response missingness, critical misunderstanding cases, no artificial retention claims. Any estimate of audio-vs-text difference is restricted to the tested voices, population and design.

## R163 transfer boundary

R163 studied US Mechanical Turk health-information passages in text vs **default male-US Azure TTS**, n=274 text and 213 audio across two parallel conditions. Multiple tasks per worker and no duplicate-content crossover were described, while **randomization procedure was not explicitly reported**. The reported MC/TF comprehension and free-recall measures are neither a human-voice script intervention nor YouTube retention. Do not read F-190 into K031 as support, nor reuse R163 for K011, K019, K029 or K044 positive efficacy.

## Readiness, cost and stops

No human recruitment, audio recording, participant compensation, or paid provider is authorized under this stage. Future approvals must determine ethics review requirements, privacy/deletion policy, disability access accommodations, staff budget, sample-size justification, validated questions and preregistration. Stop on invalid consent, distress, diagnosis risk, inaccessible instructions, unintelligible audio, unauthorized excerpts or unsupported original video audio.
