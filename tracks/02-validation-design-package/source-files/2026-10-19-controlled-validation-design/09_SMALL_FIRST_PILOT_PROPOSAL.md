# Deliverable 9 — Small First-Pilot Proposal (NOT EXECUTED)

**Proposal name:** A1 — Four-Category Source-and-Editorial Feasibility Pilot. **Type:** independent expert/desk review; no listeners, recordings, YouTube releases, analytics pulls, source discovery, paid providers or writing-skill implementation.

## Objective and exclusions

Demonstrate that the *proposed review process* can reproducibly reject factual and ethical distortions while differentiating four category-specific editorial qualities. It cannot test YouTube retention, recommendations, actual audio, genuine audience persuasion or cross-linguistic behavioral improvement. No general production instruction will be produced from an editorial rubric pass.

## Proposed sample construction (requires separate operational approval)

- 4 strata: A01 German war-history emotional reporting; A02 English aviation/technical explanation; A05 English US weather/news services; A06 English psychology/education.
- 2 distinct creator-owned/permissioned test subjects per stratum, preferably unlike each other (e.g., personal versus collective history; airframe physics versus procurement; imminent advisory versus long-range uncertainty; mechanism study versus reflective educational framing). **8 case packets** in total.
- If approved, each case may have a *neutral factual comparator and one carefully bounded candidate variant* (16 short texts). Do not copy original V captions wholesale or replicate a competitor script. Keep facts identical within a pair, and never use an unsafe hazard claim as a public-facing test.
- Attach original primary evidence for each atom, timestamps/as-of-date, rights, category intent, language, and whether any visual meaning is essential. Sample every critical fact and up to 20 additional material atomic claims per case (record actual n).
- Add four to eight explicitly labeled **negative or nonapplicable fixtures** across categories. They are quality-assurance traps, not candidate effectiveness samples; do not use them for audience exposure.

## Preregister before execution

Pre-freeze raw case selection, variant authorship separation, order/coding/masking, rubrics, critical severity definitions, source locators, permitted N/A category handling, threshold changes, staff availability, analysis and archive retention. Report exclusions and not-checkables. Score without peeking at a second judge's decisions. Use the same category source set for each within-case contrast and add legitimate nonapplicable controls.

## Proposed interpretation and stop criteria

**Positive feasibility:** all cases source/rights cleared, zero severe misinformation misses, factual sampling and review agreement interpretable, four rubrics applied without forcing universal script timing or format rules, all counterexamples recorded, language limitations stated. **Concern/return:** low agreement, easy-negative control cueing, broad mismatch between categories, significant material errors, problematic German meaning changes. **HOLD:** lacking source/rights/qualified experts. **ABORT:** any high-severity real-risk content or ethics breach.

The provisional decision screen (zero severe errors; all high-stakes claims traced; ≥95% ordinary factual support; weighted editorial ≥75; no category subdimension <2; raw critical-label agreement ≥80% with uncertainty) is **proposed for discussion**, not a test result or validation claim. With eight cases, any precision estimate is exploratory; failure to detect harm is not proof of safety for all future scripts.

## Budget and gate

Illustrative 30–59 staff person-hours, target USD 0 service spend, no participant reimbursement because none are enrolled. Experienced local category and language reviewers must *actually be available*; otherwise case or entire pilot is a rights/expertise HOLD. Director signoff to execute is **not given in this proposal**. The existing project ends here pending the exact checkpoint in Deliverable 10.
