# Deliverable 8 — Feasibility, Budget, Rights & Access Requirements

The accompanying CSV distinguishes A expert desk, B consented listeners, C owner Analytics and D unavailable-rights/media routes. This document proposes **no purchased services** and **no human test execution**. All time and monetary amounts are planning assumptions, not invoices or completed work.

## Small first pilot (A, only after permission)

- Candidate scope: 8 short case packets (2 per category), with permissioned fact sheets and optionally 2 factual-equivalent alternatives each; no published third-party scripts copied into test media.
- **Illustrative staff effort:** 30–59 person-hours across source/rights checking, factual claim coding, editorial rubrics, independence/calibration, native DE/EN review and limited accessibility desk check. (The line-item range from CSV overlaps some work units; avoid double-counting and treat the roll-up as rough, not an engineering estimate.) This is not zero labor cost.
- **External subscription/API spend:** target **USD 0**, contingent on using owned/accessible materials and pre-existing staff. Historical archive retrieval, licensed footage, specialized reviewers or source translation **may make a case infeasible at that ceiling**; they cannot be silently procured.
- **Human participants:** 0. **Public uploads:** 0. **Audio recordings:** 0. **Analytics credentials:** 0. Any such action requires a new approval gate.

## Rights preconditions

Rights per test case must distinguish: author/origin, content owner, actual license, whether a passage may be quoted for private analysis, derivative translation rights, voice recording/replay, accessibility adaptation, geographic permissions, redistribution within the reviewing team, and retention/deletion arrangements. If a source is publicly visible, this **does not** automatically authorize copying full transcripts, video, audio or protected images. Record link/restricted excerpt rather than reproducing media without clearance.

Consult R149/R150 accessibility guidance with its original scope. R133 wildlife editing principles do not provide historical reconstruction rights; R052 inaccessible article is not licensed or substantively checked. Do not fetch additional primary content as if source extraction were authorized by this test-design assignment.

## Blocker logic

**A:** existing research records alone suffice for **design validation and source-role QA**; actual script scoring needs qualified reviewers and lawful case texts. **B:** accessibility users and representative listeners need informed consent and an approved participant budget/ethics plan. **C:** owner data and valid design are prerequisites; do not request secrets or exports in this stage. **D:** missing original player native cues, copyrighted media, original audio or underlying evidentiary source means blocked; no timing estimation or replacement with synthetic audio.
