# Deliverable 4 — Factuality & Source-Integrity Test Protocol

**Primary:** T01 source/rights gate; T02 atomic factuality review; T11 adversarial stops. **Supporting:** T08 weather as-of-date; T09 research-mechanism check; P02/P03/P04.

## Sample and units (proposed only)

Each of four categories supplies **two distinct permissioned source-bounded cases** (8 in total) with script material *only if authorized*. Build a source ledger before any alternative writing treatment. Each claim is the unit of verification; cases, authors, channels and source families are separate clustering units. A proposed sampling plan checks every critical claim and up to 20 additional ordinary material assertions per case, recording count and sampling method. If a case has fewer, review all; never force the sample count. Each claim keeps a source ID, exact locator and as-of date (or NOT_CHECKABLE).

## Positive evidence and quarantines

The repaired synthesis K-support fields are **relationships**, not independent re-verifications of every assertion. Trace actual line/location for each case through original source material. F-190 and R052 must appear only as quarantined/non-support links; R163 is not a psychology mechanism source. Source depth `index` or `excerpt` must never be silently upgraded. A user-supplied source's license, original author and right to redistribute are independently logged.

## Scoring decision tree

1. Atomize factual/date/entity/number, quote, causal mechanism, as-of forecast, speculation, implied diagnosis and re-enactment claims; preserve surrounding words/hedges.
2. Match each atom against **contemporaneous** legal primary documentation where required; score `SUPPORTED`, `SUPPORTED_WITH_QUALIFICATION`, `UNSUPPORTED`, `CONTRADICTED`, `NOT_CHECKABLE` and severity `0/1/2/3`. Unsupported or NOT_CHECKABLE high-impact assertions fail the entry gate until resolved.
3. Distinguish a quotation from paraphrase/inferred inner speech, award from flight readiness, model run from public advisory, observed study association from established cause, speaker narration from unavailable audio.
4. Freeze two independent review forms *before* disagreement discussion; preserve both raw ratings. Adjudicator then records a resolution with source locator, not a revised historical F verdict.
5. Produce all denominator counts, missing/unknown categories, severity distributions and error fixes. Do not delete a failed case from the denominator to make a percentage look better.

## Decision thresholds — proposals, not results

All severity-3 claims (immediate service harm, invented high-stakes quotations, serious private/survivor harm, falsified scientific claim) require direct confirmation or exclusion, and the reviewed pilot must contain **zero severity-3 factual errors** for an accept-for-further-testing disposition. All high-stakes claims must be traceable; ≥95% of sampled ordinary material atoms must be supportable or appropriately qualified. Missing source packet means `HOLD_NOT_ASSESSABLE`, not pass. Adversarial known-serious negative fixtures should all be identified; missing one triggers protocol redesign. Threshold choice is a Research Director question.

## Concrete negative controls

**History:** invented date/letter/eyewitness inner thought. **Aviation:** contract announcement portrayed as operational capability; false numerical speed constraint. **Weather:** historic forecast stripped of issue timestamp, model confidence expressed as certainty, wrong warning geography. **Psychology:** one correlational study presented as a universal personal causation or therapy. Each fixture is marked `TEST_ONLY`, not published as reporting.

## Risks and stop conditions

Source scarcity, dependent citations to one publisher, unstable advisory revisions, annotation granularity and masked reviewer awareness are recorded confounds. Rights clearance and consent to review user-owned material come first. Stop upon any unresolvable high-severity claim, privacy harm, source-role mispromotion or attempt to launder quarantined evidence; pass/fail threshold cannot override hard stop. **No audits have been carried out in this deliverable.**
