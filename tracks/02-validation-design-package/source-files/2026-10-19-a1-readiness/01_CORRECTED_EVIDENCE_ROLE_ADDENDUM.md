## Two medium-specific observation walls

1. **Text-level speakability only:** an expert may review sentence length, referent clarity, pronounceability of names, terminology and likely breath-group complexity *on paper*, while tagging judgments as `TEXT_LEVEL_PREDICTION`. `ACTUAL_AUDIO_PROSODY` must be `NOT_OBSERVED` because no permissioned audio evaluation is in A1. “Spoken clarity” rubric labels in the older design should be interpreted as **anticipated text speakability**, not measured delivery.
2. **Storyboard semantic mapping only:** with a lawfully supplied storyboard, evaluate whether textual references *could* be matched to the planned visual and whether vital facts are available in words. `AUDIOVISUAL_SYNC`, cue timing, on-screen behavior and accessibility of a rendered file stay `NOT_OBSERVED`. Do not convert W3C standards text into a pass for unexamined final media.

## Source-level inclusion rule

`F` = historical source-faithfulness/governance or unverified/quarantined where flagged; `R` = scoped norm/resource/official guidance unless published empirical design explicitly verified; `V` = **descriptive caption text only**. R079 and R163 are the only bounded empirical *context* relationships identified here, not tests of A1 variants. No source in this package measures a causal change in YouTube retention from these research candidates.

The row-by-row addendum prevents semantic promotion while keeping every previous historic source identity, ID, verdict and linkage unchanged. No new empirical or source-reading verdict is asserted.
