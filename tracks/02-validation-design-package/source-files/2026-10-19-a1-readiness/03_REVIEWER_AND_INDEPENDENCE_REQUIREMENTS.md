# 3. Reviewer and Independence Requirements — staffing not verified

No roster exists in the accepted package. **Zero reviewers or adjudicators are verified as engaged.** This is a staffing specification; it does not recruit anyone.

## Minimum distinct functions (subject to conflict screening)

- **Eight blinded category raters**: for each category, two independently qualified editors or domain reviewers score each of its two cases; raters must not have authored either version or supervised its preparation. German historical reviewers must be capable of *native-level German* and historical source criticism. English aviation requires technical/procurement expertise; US weather must include operational hazard literacy; psychology must include research-methods and non-diagnostic judgment.
- **One independent bilingual DE↔EN meaning reviewer** for optional translation-transfer material, plus a separate competent source/target reviewer. A translator or drafter cannot sign off on their own translation. If a meaning-equivalent pair cannot lawfully be provided, DE/EN transfer is `DEFERRED_NOT_ASSESSABLE` without holding ordinary single-language desk appraisal hostage.
- **One reserved adjudicator**, separate from two pre-lock judges and with category-specialist referral for disputed technical/factual judgments. Never count adjudication as an independent raw rating.
- **One masking/data steward** may be the research coordinator but must not rate; records raw versions, random order and the decoding key. Cross-role reuse is allowed only if it does **not** destroy blind independence and the hour ledger discloses it.

These are **role slots**, not verified people. Potentially 11 named roles including steward; distinct headcount may be lower if conflicts are demonstrably excluded. Expertise availability, compensation, owner approvals, reviewer contact, language proficiency and institution/financial conflicts are **all unverified**.

## Assignment and locking procedure, after authorization only

1. An independent case-material steward confirms source packets, permission ledger and case exclusions. Assign masked pair IDs without revealing candidate/comparator status or author to either rater. Category/context and factual source material remain known so judgment is meaningful.
2. Require dated signed disclosure: authorship, common employer or project incentives, shared model output/prompt, preexisting access to the candidate, co-review discussions, financial stake, content ownership and language fluency.
3. Two raters per category evaluate both of that category's cases *independently*. If a rater recognizes a script or source, flags potential unblinding; the coordinator predefines replacement/exclusion. No group calibration using test cases or exchanging in-progress ratings.
4. Record itemized ordinal 0–4 rubric judgments, atomic factual severity and `N/A`, `NOT_ASSESSABLE`, `NOT_OBSERVED` separately. A style score cannot override a critical factual or rights failure.
5. Freeze and timestamp raw independent forms, report raw agreement and numerator/denominator before reasoned adjudication. Chance-adjusted coefficients are exploratory with tiny samples; never present agreement as retention validation.
6. Only **after** the frozen first pass can adjudicator examine identities and specific conflicts, preserving both original ratings and version metadata. Revised calibration/rubric becomes a new numbered round; no silent retroactive editing.

## Language boundaries

A1 German history requires reviewers who can distinguish respectful historical German from an unjustified universal `du`/`Sie` prohibition (F-092 failed). Names and compound clauses can be evaluated **as text speakability predictions**, but actual voice pacing/intonation is `NOT_OBSERVED`. English reviewers need category-appropriate terminology; bilingual review is **meaning equivalence only**, not audience performance transfer. No one may certify actual audio delivery or onscreen synchronization from paper.
