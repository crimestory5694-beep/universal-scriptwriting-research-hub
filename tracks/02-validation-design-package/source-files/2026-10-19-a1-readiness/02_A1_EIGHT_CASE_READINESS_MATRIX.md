`READY` requires affirmative, auditable evidence for every hard gate: final two authorized scripts/versions per case, primary factual materials with locators and as-of date, clear permission to make/score comparison material, two actually available independent qualified reviewers, reserved adjudicator, safe conditions, and a locked review plan. No A1 row meets that bar. `CONDITIONAL` means a feasible path is conceivable *after* Director authorization of preparation and actual verification; it **does not** permit testing. `BLOCKED` is for a slot that would require prohibited access/media, unauthorized rights use or cannot be made safe within scope. No current slot inherently needs barred original-player footage or private Analytics, but reliance on them would immediately make that proposed path BLOCKED.

## Primary factual/source packets that would be required

- **German history H01/H02:** catalog-verifiable original dated archival records and contemporaneous corroboration; quote/translation attribution and chronological reconciliation; permissions for source excerpts. Neither V01/V02/V03 nor BBC wildlife R133 grants such rights.
- **Aviation A01/A02:** dated authoritative engineering/manufacturer/certification information or official procurement award and separately documented delivery/in-service milestones. Captions V04–V06 are not technical primary proof.
- **Weather W01/W02:** preserved original issued advisory or seasonal probabilistic outlook, issue/valid date, time zone, geographic coverage, and update history; make the historical-not-live framing unambiguous. V07–V09 captions do not supersede as-issued agency data.
- **Psychology P01/P02:** original peer-reviewed mechanism evidence when assertions are empirical; distinguish a nonempirical reflective essay from verified psychology. R079 and R163 are **domain-mismatched** for claims about a general viewer treatment, narrator cadence or a script’s retention gain.

For every slot, optional storyboards may support **semantic referent checks only**; no audio or rendered video is presumed. The CSV gives each slot’s full source, rights, expertise, fixture, blinding and status inventory. No scripts or variants have been drafted in this phase.
