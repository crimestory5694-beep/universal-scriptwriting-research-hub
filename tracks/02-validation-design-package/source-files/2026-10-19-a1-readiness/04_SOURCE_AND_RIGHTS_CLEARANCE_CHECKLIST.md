# 4. Source and Rights Clearance Checklist

**128 scoped checklist cells**: 8 proposed case slots × 16 source/rights controls. **64 required verifications still outstanding**, **40 optional feature-specific controls requiring explicit exclusion or clearance if used**, and **24 correctly not applicable across other categories**. None is presented as cleared. The companion CSV gives per-case proof requirements and applicability rather than inaccurately treating weather, aviation, psychology and history checks as mandatory for every slot.

### Legal and content hygiene gates

**First establish authored case identity, then asset-by-asset rights.** Public display, government publisher, “educational,” or short excerpt alone does not prove permission to create anonymized derivative comparison texts. For first-phase desk rating, secure lawful text-access and internal sharing; do not presume permission to publish, synthesize narration, use maps, screen-record, translate or redistribute after analysis.

Separate (a) original scripts/comparators, (b) underlying primary records, (c) caption extracts, (d) storyboard/graphics, and (e) audio/video. Each has its own owner, context and legal restrictions. **No new audio/video or clips are needed for A1.** W3C R149/R150 provide accessibility guidance, not copyright or experimental consent.

**Publisher examples:** R156 is an SEC gallery, not clearance for embedded videos; R157 is an SEC guide but marks/external content need asset review; R158/R159 are CFPB portals with unexamined linked works. R133 is a historical BBC wildlife document and does not authorize making human-history reconstruction. R052 remains unaccessed and unrelated to clearance.

### Primary-source threshold per category

- H01/H02: source catalog identifier/date, contemporaneous corroboration where available, quote provenance, historically fair uncertainty, privacy/dignity screening.
- A01/A02: original signed specification/manual/reliable government procurement/fielding source, as-of cut-off and distinction between capabilities, procurement, deliveries and operational service.
- W01/W02: verified original weather agency dated issue/expiration/version and affected locations/time zones; the example must be clearly historical so no viewer mistakes it for live advice.
- P01/P02: real, read original empirical source when scientific mechanisms are asserted; separate reflection from evidence and exclude unsupported diagnosis/personal-treatment assertions.

### Reviewer and data rights

Only reviewer-team internal, permissioned access is contemplated. Verify confidentiality, access permissions, deletion window, conflict disclosures and permission to quote in reports. Rights granted for *reading* are not necessarily rights for *translation or adaptation*. Pending means `UNKNOWN`, not cleared; any nonresolvable rights concern = case `BLOCKED` under the contemplated scope.

**This checklist is a request for future verified documentation, not a declaration of legal compliance.** No rights, reviewer, archival, or source clearance was obtained in this readiness-design phase.
