# 6. Pre-registration and Blinding Checklist

This document names what **must be frozen before** the first pilot action. It is not an executed preregistration, a random assignment, a reviewer invitation, a fixture, or an observation. Every item remains pending; see the companion 22-row CSV.

## Plan to preregister only after Director authorizes preparation

**Cases & consent:** exact eight case IDs matched to actual subject and owner, title/content hashes, two legally reviewed text versions per subject, date limits, source locators, eligibility criteria and withdrawal/replacement rule. Register right-to-transform separately from right-to-read. If only one version is offered or original rights are unclear, that case remains on hold.

**Review quantities:** two category-qualified independent review forms per case; every critical statement sourced plus up to 20 ordinary atomic claims per case (actual population n reported); ordinal 0–4 category dimensions with original accepted weights; factual/ethics pass is a separate non-compensable gate. Distinguish `NOT_CHECKABLE`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `NOT_OBSERVED` and `MISSING` from a numerical zero. With only 8 cases, no reliable category-wise causal inference or industry-wide performance validation is possible.

**A1 rubric references:** retain the accepted four-rubric dimensions *unchanged*. Label the operational interpretation `A1-RUBRIC-MAPPING-v0.1 (PROPOSED, NOT LOCKED)`: English/German *text speakability*, not demonstrated speech; planned visual semantic referents, not AV synchronization. Scoring order, analyst calculations, weights, severity criteria and exclusions must be signed by the Director and frozen before any rater sees material.

**Counterexamples/fixtures:** reserve four-to-eight distinct, context-specific valid-negative or nonapplicable examples, across four categories, for calibration only. They must be authored or licensed **after explicit execution/material preparation approval**; none is included in this package. Verify that source errors (fabricated quote, award counted as delivery, expired hazard presented as current, false psychology mechanism) are *detectable* without turning participant exposure into unsafe media. A legitimate no-mystery, collective history, direct service alert or simple taxonomic explanation must be allowed to score well where appropriate. Never prelabel the live test cases as easy negatives to raters.

**Masking:** non-rater holds author/version crosswalk; masked IDs label items; category and neutral source evidence must remain visible; order within balanced pair presentation is randomized or counterbalanced using a frozen reproducible rule; maintain recognition/guess flag. Distinct primary authors for versions or visible co-author conflicts are documented and managed. Raters do not see each other's forms prior to timestamped lock. Unmask only for reserved adjudicator after raw agreement calculation; retain disagreement pre- and post-adjudication.

**Stop and downgrade:** absent permission/source/qualified reviewers = case `CONDITIONAL`/`BLOCKED`, **not** silent substitution. Unmasked candidate ID, unanticipated legal ownership, serious live-risk misinformation, misrepresented German meaning, unverifiable high-impact claims or reviewer distress trigger pause/escalation. All material changes require new version and Director signoff; do not retroactively edit the preregistered protocol.

**No execution:** no materials were generated, human ratings solicited, fixtures written, participants enrolled, videos/analytics accessed, or sample assignment randomized.
