# 4. Reviewer roles, qualifications and independence

## Required staffing (roles, not current people)

- Two suitably qualified **human** independent reviewers in each of the four categories (minimum eight rater identities if two experts share the category's two cases); a reserved independent adjudicator and a non-scoring masking/data steward. Conditional DE↔EN transfer would add independent bilingual source/target expertise and licensed translations.
- **H01 and H02:** proficient historical German (near-native fluency plus archival source criticism); WW2 political history for H01 and WWI/naval/translation provenance for H02. Shared history experts only if both subdomains genuinely demonstrated.
- **A01 and A02:** specialized propulsion/airworthiness and acquisition/program-status knowledge; plain-English technical editor; no substitution of general copywriters for specialists.
- **W01 and W02:** US NWS hazard/forecast service knowledge versus long-range probabilistic seasonal outlook literacy; at least one practiced meteorologist per case.
- **P01 and P02:** research-methods and empirical psychology boundaries versus ethical reflective narrative; empirical claim checking cannot be delegated to unsupported AI consensus.

## Quantities, availability and conflicts

Two first-pass raters per case; initially assume 16 case-rating assignments across 8 cases, by 8 qualified category raters where valid. Plan **~1.5–3.5 human hours per rater per case** as a workload sensitivity (included inside, not added to, the previously costed review T03/T10 work). Additional check-specific work remains in the accepted plan. No person, availability window, rate, credential, independence or conflict form has been verified or solicited.

Authors, editors who prepared a text, case owners, people with material outcome-based incentives, and people who have previewed paired identities are ineligible for blind raw rating absent an explicit conflict rule. Adjudicator does not count as an independent initial rater; language reviewers may have distinct roles but cannot approve their own translation; coordinator holds mask key and does not rate. Multiple AI agents are not independent human experts. Do not convene reviewers or pre-register scores in M0.
