# 2. Ownership and permission gaps

**Zero owner attestations, zero test grants, zero approved matched comparator versions.** All eight case permissions are open. The gap register contains six distinct obligations per case. File access is not an assignment of IP ownership; collaboration, contracted writing, archive quotations, included graphics and translations may have separate rights.

**Needed receipt fields:** case ID; original manuscript version and checksum; named author(s), rightsholder(s) and if relevant employer/client; provenance and permissions for coauthored and AI-assisted passages; express limited confidential editorial review and storage grant; expiry, revocation and deletion rules; authorized reviewer geography and sharing; excerpt/quote restrictions; archival or publisher clearance; separate comparator license and provenance. No grant, consent, or acceptance has been inferred.

**Unusable substitutes:** V01–V12 competitor captions, ripped third-party narration, inferred public-domain books, archive catalogue abstracts republished as text, unlicensed screenshots, original-owner Analytics, or automatically assumed permissions from chat/file uploads.
