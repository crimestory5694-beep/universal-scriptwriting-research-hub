# Remaining evidence gaps after Batch 2

1. **No controlled writing manipulation:** R079 is large but observational. Need random assignment of script-only variants holding visuals, voice, topic/title and delivery fixed, with preregistered metrics, uncertainty and negative outcomes.
2. **No script-text attribution:** Most production evidence concerns packaging/format, not actual transcript wording, spoken pauses or writing choices. No approved source asserts writing independently caused viral success.
3. **Finance video content unexamined:** R156/R158 offer video catalog indexes; no individual published video content, author draft, script or voice was read. Later request permission per asset.
4. **BBC current guidance:** R133 original link blocked; exact same-title BBC-hosted archival PDF from 2012 was read. Confirm current BBC policy in an ordinary future authorized source review, do not assume temporal validity.
5. **Shorts comparability:** Require dates, post-Aug 2026 view definitions, engaged and qualified metrics, format, publication age, exposure, and owned-channel analytics for causal questions.
6. **Language/accessibility:** English-focused primary sources, W3C translation guidance not a German/Urdu audience experiment. Need native-language listener comprehension and disability accommodations.
7. **Sports delivery:** R141 guidance designed for live play-by-play, not edited analysis. Need independent original sports-production studies or a controlled audio/radio listener study.
8. **Rights:** BBC, STAA and ACM copyright; CFPB third-party assets; SEC Investor.gov video and YouTube hosting asset rights; W3C document no-derivatives terms.
9. **Empirical uncertainty:** R079 shows some approximate 95% CI graphics and p-values, but no universal pooled causal effect size; need comparable clips matched on subject/instructor/viewer.
10. **Source family correlation:** R149/R150 W3C and R098/R160/R161 YouTube add complementary definitions, not new empirical families.
