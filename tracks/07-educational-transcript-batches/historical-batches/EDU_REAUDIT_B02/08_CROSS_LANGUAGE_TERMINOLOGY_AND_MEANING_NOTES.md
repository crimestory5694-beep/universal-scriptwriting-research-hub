## Translation method and ambiguity

- Hindi educational creators often borrow English words (`hook`, `stakes`, `gap`, `retention`) and inflect them with Hindi grammar; retain the English technical sense while preserving creator-specific wording.
- Several Hindi transcripts have inconsistent ASR spellings or malformed numeric cues. Original source text remains authoritative evidence only for *what the transcript records*, not verified speech or scientific findings.
- The word “science” and named psychological effects in a video are not sufficient to establish scientific validity.
- Different-language teaching that expresses similar ideas is not automatically a translated version of the same video.
- Other source languages and source-equivalence mapping remain pending for unreviewed documents.
