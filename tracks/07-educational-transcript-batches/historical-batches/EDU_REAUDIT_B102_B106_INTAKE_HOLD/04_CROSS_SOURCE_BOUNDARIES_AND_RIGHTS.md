1. **Multiple sources are not a validation experiment.** Repeated marketing explanations cannot establish cognitive/psychological causality, CTR or retention gains.
2. `0105` named individual biography, sponsor placement, and alleged theory are source attribution only. No independent identity/metrics check, and no causal conclusion from narrated persona changes.
3. `0106` thumbnail ideas are adjacent packaging research, not universal script prose rules. Visible comparison figures, alleged spend and eye-scanning tests are not authenticated from caption text.
4. `0107` wealth and brain science claims are not reliable financial/medical advice or scientific psychology evidence.
5. `0109` paid cloud voice/face clones require authorization, identity safeguards, secure API handling, and current platform-policy verification. No cloned footage is published here. Its synthetic English ending is an in-video demo.
6. `0110` VIRAL is a creator's mnemonic and context-dependent short-video model. Its short-video speed rules, timing, certainty about algorithms or long-form B-plot requirements must not become mandatory instructions for historical or weather narration.
7. Unverified original A/V, captions and malformed time cues are not repaired. A source timestamp such as :60 is original transcript text, not independently corrected time.
