## Additional safety-sensitive pitfalls (analyst review; NOT creator-taught universal rules)

- Withholding an answer in narrative nonfiction must not turn into misrepresentation of a known outcome.
- Body language, exact dialogue and internal thoughts should be source-grounded for actual historical people.
- Full-script versus bullet speaking method is conditional on format; a faceless evidence-heavy script is not automatically better when improvised.
- Claims of dopamine, oxytocin, neural coupling, 8-second attention and YouTube algorithm thresholds require independent source verification before scientific or operational use.
- The creator who suggests mid-video likes/comments is not an authority to override a production channel’s no-mid-CTA policy.
- Screenplay abbreviations may differ by format/producer; do not silently impose a claimed OC/OS distinction as an industry fact.
