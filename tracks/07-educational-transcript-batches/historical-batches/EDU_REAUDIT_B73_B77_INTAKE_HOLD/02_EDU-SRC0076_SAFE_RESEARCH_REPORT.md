## Source-based research synthesis

The long course separates talking-head stories, immersive short form, longer mini-documentaries and on-camera delivery. The talking-head section teaches CAT (Context–Adversity–Takeaway), optionally CARD with a resolution; PAST (Place–Action–Speech–Thoughts); and three story-selection types: coaching, conversion and catalyst. Short form adds Mission–Progression–Payoff and four S ideas: Surprise, Stakes, Show, Simplicity. Long form expands a mission into context, plan, progression, climax and reflection, with questions/contrasts as editorial devices. Delivery instruction introduces improvised daily practice and six speaking styles.
