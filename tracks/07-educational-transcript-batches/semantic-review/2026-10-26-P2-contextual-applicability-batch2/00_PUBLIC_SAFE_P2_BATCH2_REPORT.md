## Decision-level synthesis

1. **No reconstructed thoughts or near-miss dialogue presented as documentary fact.** Several creator lessons explicitly use hypothetical bosses, imagined family stories, invented sales-call counts, war-scene analogies or approximated speech. Such devices can remain clearly labeled fictional teaching examples but must never be reported as real history.
2. **Anecdotes require original source confirmation.** Celebrity-family and corporate anecdotes, lottery stories, volunteer recollections, career biographies and YouTube 3,000-view/47-million-view claims remain third-party or self-reported without independent primary verification. Repetition across creators is not corroboration.
3. **CAT, PAST, character goals and visual-first openings are OPTIONAL creator-taught structural candidates**, not empirically established retention prescriptions. Factual weather, military and historical story structure must respect geography, chronology, uncertainty and original evidence.
4. **Media editing, generative video software, voice recording, CPM claims, sponsorship placement and title packaging** belong to production/market/packaging modules, not universal narration rules.
5. **Reusable AI skills and handoff tutorials** can inform implementation design, but platform procedures need up-to-date documentation and client data/security/rights controls. Independent policy crosswalk: Associated Press news principles require accurate quotations and no manufactured reporting (https://www.ap.org/about/news-values-and-principles/telling-the-story/).
