# Publication and validation boundary

This public checkpoint publishes analyst-written findings and non-identifying QA counts, **not** restricted row-by-row source paraphrases, verbatim third-party captions, caption-byte offsets, actual private evidence ZIPs, original copyrighted teaching material, or unrecovered source-rich ledgers.

100 separate Batch 2 review decisions are stored in the private archive with exact source-qualified keys and recorded researcher cautions. Review is text-based contextual transfer assessment; empirical retention improvements and factual anecdotes remain unverified without independent tests.

For production use: no invented quotes or thoughts about real persons, no artificially precise statistical claims, no implication that a narrative technique causes viewing retention, and no realistic AI event reenactment without relevant disclosure/context gates.
