## Findings from source-grounded editorial judgment
- The creator examples of deadline-driven stakes, surprise, mission resolution, Dan Harmon story circle, open questions and direct speech are **optional teaching methods**, not controlled experimental effects. Weather and military documentary facts must preserve forecast uncertainty, actual chronology and unresolved outcomes. Movie countdowns, invented exchanges, fictional marathon motivation and the 'Buffett sole-car' thought experiment may not become factual reporting.
- Interview memory prompts may gather testimony but do not independently establish its accuracy. Self-reported channel metrics, virality-stage theory, 'viewer-comment research', advertiser assumptions, niche persona, algorithm identifiers and presenter anxiety exercises cannot be stated as proven universal advice.
- A caption-based lesson about thumbnails cannot authenticate images shown onscreen, focal point perception or results. Title/thumbnail alignment is a packaging design question to test with trustworthy analytics; it does not measure narration retention by itself.
- Tool/agent skills, AI avatar consistency, vendor subscriptions, ElevenLabs voice cloning and push notifications require live-version verification, appropriate likeness rights, clear synthetic-content disclosure and restricted credential storage. A handoff note does not certify remote completion.
