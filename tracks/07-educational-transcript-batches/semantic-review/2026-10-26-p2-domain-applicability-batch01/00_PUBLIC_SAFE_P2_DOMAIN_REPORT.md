## Documentary transfer boundaries
- **USA weather / disaster:** Present official hazards, timing, geography, uncertainty and decision-relevant impacts; narrative suspense cannot justify withholding safety information. NWS describes why forecast uncertainty must be communicated. https://www.weather.gov/about/forecast-process
- **Military / aviation / geostrategy:** Verified chronology and mechanism explanations may use clear story transitions, but do not invent pilots' dialogue, report unsupported battlefield outcomes or pass off illustrated cutaways as real combat footage. Journalism attribution guidance: https://www.ap.org/about/news-values-and-principles/telling-the-story/
- **German WW1/WW2 human history:** Use letters, diaries, interview records and attributed archive sources for alleged speech and thoughts; hypothetical creator teaching dialogue is not a documentary quotation. Documentary third-party-material standard: https://www.pbs.org/standards/accuracy/
- **Skill and workflow tutorials:** Reference files, explicit output formats, mock tests, brand constraints and separated project data are potential Claude Code architecture aids, not evidence that scripts will attract a given retention rate.
