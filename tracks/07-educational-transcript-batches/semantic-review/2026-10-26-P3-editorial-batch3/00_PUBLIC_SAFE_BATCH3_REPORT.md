## Editorial findings
1. **Specific detail must be true:** A reported weather location, aircraft pilot's intention, historic quote, remembered sound or witness inner thought must have real source evidence. Hypothetical manager confrontations, self-talk lines and wedding speeches in courses do not become historical dialogue.
2. **Structural candidates not laws:** Clear stakes, verified progress and setbacks, optional questions and a relevant evidence-bounded ending are reasonable editorial trials. The research does not establish a mandatory identity-change arc, conflict escalation or fixed-second retention formula; unfinished forecasts and wars cannot receive invented resolutions.
3. **Creator anecdotes not independent data:** Self-reported course spending, storytelling skill rankings, celebrity board decisions, viral reach, subscriber loyalty, earnings and recommendation-system changes need original records before becoming documentary factual claims or success predictions.
4. **Rights and source dignity:** Disclosing trauma must remain voluntary; impersonating restaurant workers is not an endorsed creator exercise. Republished film footage, copied videos, celebrity scenes and synthetic voices require appropriate rights and clear attribution.
5. **Tool/packaging separate from script evidence:** Captions cannot authenticate generated image consistency, current PDF-upload interface features, vendor costs, or a thumbnail experiment's winning variant. AI Skills tutorials need present product, access and security checks.
