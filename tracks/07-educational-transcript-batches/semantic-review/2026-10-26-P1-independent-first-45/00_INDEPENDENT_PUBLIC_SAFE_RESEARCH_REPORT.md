## Independently corroborated findings with limits

- **Anxiety reappraisal:** Alison Wood Brooks's peer-reviewed experiment exists and addresses pre-performance anxiety in tasks including public speaking. It is *not* a YouTube narration-retention or universal scripting-effectiveness trial. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24364682/
- **Learning pyramid:** Fixed 5/10/50/75/90 percent teaching-method memory claims are not scientifically established as universal measured values; the historical/medical-education critique documents the evidentiary problem. https://asmepublications.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/medu.13813
- **Recommendations and retention thresholds:** Official YouTube documentation describes personalized recommendations, audience appeal, engagement and satisfaction. No universal 90%-in-six-seconds retention mandate, 30% baseline or rigid initial test-batch algorithm was verified. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16533387?hl=en
- **CTR context:** YouTube explicitly says impressions CTR may fall as a video reaches a broader audience, which does not authenticate a creator's own hidden-algorithm story. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16767369?hl=en
- **Thumbnail/title experiments:** Eligible creators can use YouTube Studio A/B testing of up to three title/thumbnail variations; outcomes use watch-time criteria, not a guarantee of click-lift or an editable scripting rule. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en
- **Significant Objects:** Original project reports $128.74 paid for objects and $3,612.51 in proceeds; project results do not estimate an isolated causal story effect through random assignment. https://significantobjects.com/about/
- **Kama muta:** A real academic emotion construct, being moved by love; not empirical proof that emotional stories are remembered indefinitely. https://escholarship.org/uc/item/01g0636t
