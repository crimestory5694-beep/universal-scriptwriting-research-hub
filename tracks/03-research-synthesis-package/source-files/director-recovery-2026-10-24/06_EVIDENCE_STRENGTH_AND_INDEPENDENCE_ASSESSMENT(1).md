## Two empirical cases with material external validity limits

**R079 — Guo/Kim/Rubin (2014):** original analysis of 862 educational video records and 6,902,358 viewing sessions across studied edX courses, with **127,839 course-reported student counts summed across course rows (not proven deduplicated users)**. Production characteristics and video engagement are observed, not randomized script choices. Some contexts include a **33% vs 33% null** problem-attempt comparison. Cannot set maximum video length or universal hook/payoff seconds.

**R163 — JMIR 2025:** 487 analyzed US online workers, 274 text and 213 synthesized-audio assignments for health passages; synthetic male TTS and comprehension/recall tasks. Some text–audio differences are substantial and others small or nonsignificant; modality study not a script-writing intervention and not YouTube retention.
