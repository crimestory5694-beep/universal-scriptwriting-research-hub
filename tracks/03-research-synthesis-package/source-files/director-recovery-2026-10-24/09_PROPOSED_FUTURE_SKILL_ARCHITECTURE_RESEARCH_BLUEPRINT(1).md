## Non-executable conceptual flow

**Immutable source registry → eligibility / quarantine gate → category/niche/sub-niche context selection + 8 independent axes → professional/ethical review eligibility → conditionally relevant craft observations → explicit counterexample and independence audit → research evaluation registry → Research Director checkpoint.**

This flow allows, for example, a weather service warning and a German ethical biography to share evidence provenance discipline while remaining free to use different opening/payoff designs. It does not prescribe either design; it only records what should be researched before a future authorized development stage.

## Design governance and versioning

- Link every future proposed module to S, F, R, V, or new approved append-only IDs; keep original 90 F IDs immutable.
- Preserve original historical verdict and revised conditional interpretation in separate fields.
- A source page may establish an ethical obligation, a practitioner suggestion or an observed example; it must not automatically become a measured algorithm.
- Expose uncertainty and negative cases. Access failures are data, not evidence of absence.
- Record rights state per asset. Annotated analytical paraphrase is not permission to reproduce whole copyrighted scripts, captions or diagrams.
- Stop before writing the implementation, production workflow prompts or scripts; require an explicit new director-approved work order and source/rights reconciliation.
