## Evidence-based F-125 adjudication

Authors describe many unfaithful claims involving events and character states, often requiring indirect reasoning; content-selection analysis identifies an overemphasis on late-book events. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01261 . Historical original status UNVERIFIED retained. ## F-190 adjudication: do not turn limited evidence into an absolute negative

Ahmed et al., *J Med Internet Res* 2025 (https://www.jmir.org/2025/1/e69772/) studied text vs audio health information with **487 participants**; common readability formula outcomes did not correlate with actual tested comprehension/retention, although perceived text difficulty did correlate. Original ARI validation concerned written technical material: Smith & Kincaid (1970), https://doi.org/10.1177/001872087001200505; McLaughlin SMOG 1969 indexed at https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ004016 . These support caution about using print grade levels as proxies for listening **in tested contexts**, but do NOT verify the ledger’s universal historical claim about **every** enumerated formula, or impossibility of validation for all listening. Closure remains UNVERIFIED.
