# 04 — Four Category-Specific Comparisons

## H — German emotional WW1/WW2 history (V01–V03)

**Commonality (observable):** human consequences appear in the narrated opening rather than a pure battle timeline. V01 starts with a rescue dilemma; V02 with postwar civilian conditions; V03 with a doctor facing collapse in the cold. The scripts use past/present contrast, but not one uniform act sequence.

**Divergence:** V01 builds a single sacrificial choice and delayed 1994 recognition; V02 is multi-person broadcaster/expert documentary with social-economic progression; V03 follows a memoir-linked medical witness across campaign chronology. V01 also uses an unusually early subscription request after the cold open, while V03 ends on reflection rather than a subscription bridge.

**Counterexample:** “all German wartime stories require a singular hero and tragedy” is rejected by V02, a collective postwar survival documentary with cautious reconstruction.

**Risk:** V01 supplies precise dramatic dialogue, internal motive and a 10:37 historical clock without independent source in the captions. V03’s automated transcript misspells **Heinrich Haape**; source verification required. War accounts must not replace ethical complexity with pure personal hero worship. Audience expectations and independent historical sourcing differ across channels.

**Comparability weakness:** disparate upload years (2020, 2025, 2026), brands and run times (14:37–32:18), plus institutional archive/graphics support; view counts do not isolate script craft.

## A — Aviation & Technology (V04–V06)

**Commonality:** each opens a clear technical/institutional contradiction and advances a causal explanation. V04: supersonic excellence but rapid retirement. V05: power to spare but Mach ceiling. V06: under-pressure Boeing wins two fighter programs, leaving Northrop out.

**Divergence:** V04 is component-by-component aircraft anatomy, V05 a record-event mystery closed by inlet-temperature physics, V06 a 25-minute acquisition/news narrative with numbered subparts and explicit competing stakeholders. V04 and V06 both offer tightly matched **next-video** bridges (U-2, F-47); V05 finishes with open audience suggestion instead.

**Counterexample:** a universal “start every tech video with personal peril” fails for V04 and V06, which introduce constraints and procurement stakes without a first-person emergency.

**Risk:** numerical physics must survive original manual/specification verification. News-reactive forecasts of aircraft production and rival programs should remain conditional. Boeing’s Sept 2026 F/A-XX award is corroborated, not proof of technical/program success.

**Comparability weakness:** Airspeed/Rivet 9–10 minutes vs Mach Line 25 minutes, distinct news freshness, channel sizes, niches and visual engineering exposition.

## W — US Weather & News (V07–V09)

**Commonality:** lead with **what, where and when** in the forecast. Region-day/hazard updates supply intermediate payoffs; model/forecast uncertainty is a core service obligation, not an optional suspense device.

**Divergence:** V07 combines a recent-hurricane retrospective, conversational humor, sponsor break and the coming front. V08/V09 are consecutive-day updates from one channel; their near-identical setups demonstrate repeated service-format relevance, not independent creative tests. V07 qualifies snow outcomes and mentions disagreeing model temperatures; V08 names categorical risks; V09 uses detailed daily timing.

**Counterexample:** “payoff must arrive only after a long mystery” is unsafe for public forecasts. Here actionable locations/hazards surface from the first speech paragraphs.

**Risk:** a forecast is valid as of narration/publish date only. Published SPC/WPC/NHC advisories must be archived if making factual judgments about accurate public warnings. Sponsor and radar overlays confound spoken pacing. Two same-channel updates cannot count as two independent audiences.

**Comparability weakness:** October 12–14 dates are similar, but event urgency, sponsorship, channel reach and repeated update intent differ. Public 2026 view snapshots are not watch-time.

## P — Psychology & Education (V10–V12)

**Commonality:** a relatable experience appears before, or together with, the central conceptual lens. V10/V11 address procrastination; V12 interprets fluctuating mood through one poetic analogy.

**Divergence:** V10 vignette → definition → mechanism → evidence → supportive steps. V11 comic viewer callout → brain-personification → tools and Patreon. V12 continuous weather metaphor and philosophical qualification, with almost no study citations or step-by-step technique.

**Counterexample:** “every psychology video requires a three-step hack with overt CTA” is falsified by V12. Conversely, a purely poetic analogy must not automatically be treated as neuroscience evidence.

**Risk:** claims about limbic system, willpower depletion, stress hormones and self-forgiveness deserve primary citations and nuance; a transcript does not verify scientific accuracy. Be careful not to equate tone of certainty with strength of clinical evidence.

**Comparability weakness:** dates span 2022–2025, run times span 3:38–6:21, publisher prestige and monetization differ, psychology subformat is not identical.

## Category evidence ceiling
The sample is intentionally selected, small (n=3/category), not randomly sampled, not experimental, not matched for exposure, audience, retention or impressions. Shared script conventions are **candidate descriptive patterns only**.
