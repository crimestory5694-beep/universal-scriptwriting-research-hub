# 10 — Discovery, Access, Rights and External-Source Method Notes

## Discovery chain
vidIQ connected search: German human WWII story, war recollection/biography, Mach Line/Airspeed/Rivet Count channel uploads, Ryan Hall/Max Velocity October 2026 forecasts, procrastination/science and The School of Life. Then all 12 exact IDs queried separately with the connected `yt-research-server2` detail endpoint to corroborate title/channel/date/duration and capture a public-metric snapshot.

## Captions
Access attempts used vidIQ (returned V01 German and V04 English transcripts; then credit exhaustion), `yt-research-server2` for all 12 (returned `full_transcript` and language; provider response named `supadata.ai`), Nexlev (weekly cap), Gemini research (key rejection). Only human-authored *analytical paraphrases* and narration-order anchors were saved. This package does NOT include, embed, or relicense any copyrighted complete transcripts.

Captions are original-video-associated speech data, but neither identical to audiovisual inspection nor necessarily speaker-attributed. For V02, multiple expert/narrator voices appear within unsegmented text. `caption-based` is deliberately weaker than `audio-verified`.

## Timing limit
All text-only caption responses lack `start`, `end`, segment duration or timecodes. The video's overall length is verified, but sentence-level time is not. Clock times and dates *uttered inside videos* are not elapsed-video timing. Thus every time-specific passage anchor is `NOT_AVAILABLE`, rather than being guessed.

## Public metrics
Views, likes and comments are point-in-time connector counts on 2026-10-18; the sample has very different publisher size, age, video length, audience, distribution, topic freshness and co-production. The `views/hour` provider field was deliberately left out of the register because it is a context-dependent snapshot and not listener retention.

## Rights position
YouTube URLs are public. Public availability ≠ an open license. The original copyright/licensing policy of all twelve videos was not independently retrieved or verified, and third-party caption API possession is not a grant of redistribution rights. The package contains no copyright transcript exports. Captions should not be published, reshared or treated as training data without appropriate permissions.
