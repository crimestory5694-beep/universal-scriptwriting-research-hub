# 06 — Hook, curiosity, pacing and payoff synthesis

**Measurement rule:** This is about caption-visible language and logical order; not actual speaking rate, editing cuts, waveform times, retention curves, CTR or experimental effectiveness. Openers are the first returned caption text; no exact offset can be assigned reliably.

### Hook taxonomy observed
- **Moral cost upfront:** V01 asks whether a soldier will rescue an enemy; V03 centers a doctor in frost and warfare. V02 starts with collective survival instead of a single hero.
- **Engineering or institutional contradiction:** V04 advanced bomber's brief service, V05 record plane limited by heat rather than power, V06 double procurement award and competitor loss.
- **Service warning before explanation:** V07–V09 front-load affected parts of the US, hazards and near-term timeframes. Withholding the actionable fact would be inappropriate.
- **Self-recognition or mental reframing:** V10 dramatizes deadline avoidance, V11 directly accuses the viewer of procrastinating, V12 introduces mood variability as weather.

### Curiosity mechanisms by content (not psychological efficacy claims)
V01 uses delayed moral action; V05 answers a machine-performance paradox; V07 keeps attention on forecast movement by state and city; V10 resolves why behavior that feels bad persists. These are categorically different forms of an unresolved question.

### Pacing audit and anti-generalization
There is no defensible video- or second-precise “payoff latency” or measured narration rate here: retrieved captions lack aligned timecodes. V01 introduces a subscription appeal after the hook, V07 interrupts with a sponsor read, V06 expands to numbered subsections for 25 minutes, and V12 stays in a single metaphor for fewer than four minutes. None establishes a fixed seconds-per-payoff formula.

### Where an audience promise is discharged
- V01: attempted rescue and later former-enemy memorial; the victim's survival is not demonstrated.
- V04: explains obsolescence as changed defenses and strategic mission; offers adjacent U-2 topic.
- V05: compressor inlet temperature resolves the Mach mystery, qualifying eye-catching pilot anecdotes.
- V06: win does not equal successful aircraft delivery; F-47 bridge and audience decision question.
- V07–V09: local practical regional/time guidance, then a dated update invitation. V07 explicitly reframes the cold spell as temporary.
- V10–V11: accessible practical reframes and behavior ideas; V12: closes the sky/umbrella metaphor without a typical problem-solution list.

### Interpretation ceiling
A compelling question is *observable*, not proof of higher retention. Differences in topic demand, channel size, editing, thumbnails, sponsorship, pre-existing audience, timing, traffic sources and distribution can overwhelm script wording. A/B retention tests or original analytics are needed for effectiveness.
