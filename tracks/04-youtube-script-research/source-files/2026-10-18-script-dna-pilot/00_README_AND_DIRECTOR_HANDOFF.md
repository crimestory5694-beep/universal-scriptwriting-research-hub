# Universal Scriptwriting — 12-Video YouTube Script DNA Research Pilot

**Date:** 2026-10-18 | **Decision checkpoint:** FOR RESEARCH DIRECTOR REVIEW — CONDITIONAL

### What is complete
- 12 genuine identified YouTube videos, 3 in each of History / Aviation & Technology / Weather & News / Psychology & Education.
- Original videos verified with ID, title, channel, UTC publication, duration and public count snapshots via connected vidIQ plus own yt-research server (more recent counters can vary).
- Language-matching full caption text retrieved for all 12, used internally for original, brief paraphrase-only evidence cards.
- 12 cards for all ten script DNA requested dimensions, four within-category comparisons, cross-category matrix, hook/pacing synthesis, contradictions and candidate pre-existing research links.
- Existing 171 real-world source IDs, 63 GitHub source IDs and original F findings retained intact. The pilot uses independent V01–V12 identifiers ONLY; any R/F relationships are descriptive crosswalk candidates, not approvals.

### What is NOT complete / not claimed
**No verified mm:ss narrative passage timestamps.** Transcript providers returned unsegmented text rather than timed caption cues. A third-party caption is an actual-video narration artifact but is not independent human audio verification. No source rights to reproduce entire transcripts were established. No fabricated timestamp or passage included.

**No audience retention inference.** Counts are public snapshot values, not engagement quality or evidence of viral script advantage. 12 videos are purposeful illustrative selection; NOT a matched sample or scientific evaluation.
