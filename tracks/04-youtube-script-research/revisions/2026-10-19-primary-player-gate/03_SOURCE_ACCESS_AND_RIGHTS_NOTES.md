3. No full captions, audio clips, or copyrighted media were copied into this gate package; only prior brief analytical paraphrases and public identifiers are used.

5. Ordinary direct URL and read-only open-tab inspection failed; the actual native **Show transcript** control was never visible.
6. No audio speaker attribution, transcription-error corrections, or measured spoken-delivery claims can be supported by this attempt.
